School Security Guard Service

Uniformed, disciplined security guards trained for school gate safety, student tracking, visitor screening and boundary vigil.

Key Operational Responsibilities

  • Maintain rigorous gate access control, vehicle pass register, and student entry/exit logging.
  • Verify visitor IDs and issue digital or manual visitor slips before allowing entry to campus.
  • Conduct perimeter foot patrols around sports grounds, boundaries, and administrative blocks.
  • Ensure prompt crowd de-escalation and emergency evacuation readiness in coordination with staff.

Staff Qualifications & Vetting

Police Background Verification

Every guard is scrutinized by local police authorities with clear conduct certificates.

Physical Fitness & Martial Basics

Minimum height, endurance, drill training, and fire extinguisher handling certification.
