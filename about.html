Who We Are

A Premier Security & Housekeeping Partner for Institutions

Established with a mandate to provide disciplined, fully-vetted human resource solutions, Perfect Protection Security Agency delivers specialized guarding, supervision, and housekeeping services to government schools, government offices, educational universities, healthcare complexes, factories, and commercial institutions.

We bridge the gap between institutional administrative needs and disciplined field personnel. Every guard and housekeeper deployed undergoes rigorous background checks, document validation, emergency preparedness drills, and statutory alignment including PF, ESIC, and state minimum wage acts.
