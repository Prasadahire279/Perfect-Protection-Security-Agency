How It Works: 4-Step Deployment Flow

A structured, compliant, and transparent onboarding roadmap for educational, government, and healthcare institutions.

1

Submit Requirement

Multi-Step Form Submission

Step 1 — Institutional Requirement Definition

The institution submits its specific staffing needs through our digital portal. You specify organization type (school, collectorate, hospital), choose services (security guards, housekeeping, supervisors), configure shift times, and request the exact headcount required.

  • Immediate generation of unique tracking ID (e.g. REQ-2026-XXXX).
  • No paperwork delays; automated receipt notification sent to your institutional email.
  • Direct live tracking available on the customer portal.
2

Requirement Verification

Facility Audit & SLA Agreement

Step 2 — Administrative Review & Site Assessment

Our operations division reviews the request details against statutory guidelines. A senior security inspector conducts an on-premise risk assessment of entrance gates, high-risk points, and sanitization zones.

  • Review of duty points, patrol perimeter, and shift handover protocols.
  • Verification of institutional credentials and special safety mandates.
  • Request status transitions from Pending to Approved.
3

Staff Assignment

Roster Matching & Assignment ID

Step 3 — Roster Allocation & Pre-Deployment Briefing

Using our management database, administrators assign verified guards and housekeeping staff who match the shift, language, and skill requirements of your campus. An official Assignment ID (e.g. ASG-2026-XXXX) is generated.

  • Police verification dossiers and PSARA certifications mapped to your account.
  • Supervisors briefed on emergency response, key register, and code of conduct.
  • Customer dashboard updates to display assigned personnel names and badges.
4

Service Begins

Active Deployment & Daily Oversight

Step 4 — On-Site Reporting & Daily Attendance Integrity

Personnel report on site at designated reporting times in clean formal uniforms. Daily shift attendance is logged digitally, with regular surprise checks conducted by mobile patrol field officers.

  • Daily biometric check-in and checkout logs logged into the central database.
  • Immediate 4-hour substitute guarantee if any staff member takes unscheduled leave.
  • Monthly compliance audits, EPF slips, and attendance reports delivered to administration.
